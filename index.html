Biography

Bridging Finance and Emotion

Dr. Anju Bathla Arora is a Ph.D. in Finance and a UGC-NET & JRF qualified academician with over 10 years of teaching and research experience as an Associate Professor at a leading management institute.

She is a passionate author who has written three books on personal growth, emotional intelligence, finance, and life transformation:

  • Agilent: A Book on Emotions Optimization (2018)
  • Rekindled Life (2022)
  • The Exhilarating Magic of Stock Markets (2024)

She is currently engaged in research on Emotional Intelligence Systems, exploring how emotional awareness and intelligent decision-making can help people create a more balanced, productive, and fulfilling life.
